# 0.11.0 — 2026-11-16

Breaking: `openfec_lookup_elections` rejects a ZIP search given a presidential `state` other than `US` or a presidential or Senate `district` other than `00` or `0`, and the search tools drop their bound-disclosure fields and move row-level committee records to page-level maps.

## Added

- **`openfec_search_contributions` searches itemized contributions by donor**: `contributor_id`, `contributor_name`, or `contributor_employer` scopes a search without a recipient `committee_id`, and such a page carries each recipient's committee record once, in `committees`. With none of those it fails `itemized_requires_scope` (was `itemized_requires_committee_id`), and `invalid_committee_id` names the input that carried the bad ID. ([#61](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/61))
- **Memo entries are labelled per row** in `content[]` on Schedules A, B, E, and F, and a page that mixes them with other rows counts them in its `notice`. ([#60](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/60))
- **24/48-hour notices are labelled the same way** in itemized `openfec_search_expenditures`. In the current cycle, `by_candidate` carries a `notice` naming the itemized `is_notice: true` call that lists notice-only spending, and tells a caller who passed `page` or `election_full` to leave them out. ([#59](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/59))
- **`size_range`** on `openfec_search_contributions` `by_size` rows states each bucket's dollar range; the `$200 and under` bucket includes unitemized contributions, so its count is null. ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/66))

## Changed

- **`openfec_lookup_elections` rejects** `zip` with a presidential `state` other than `US`, or a presidential or Senate `district` other than `00` or `0`, as `inputs_not_applicable_to_office`; 0.10.1 returned the race locator because `/elections/search/` ignores those filters there. ([#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/64))
- **`per_page` and `hits_returned` are honored in full**: the byte-budget cut is gone from the candidates, contributions, disbursements, expenditures, coordinated-expenditures, filings, and legal searches. `include_totals` on `openfec_search_candidates` reads every candidate's totals through a keyset walk under the call's budget, and its incomplete-totals `notice` names its three causes (budget spent, a later totals request failed, a page held no candidate's totals whole). ([#62](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/62))
- **Committee records render in full in `content[]`**, once per page, from the page-level maps `structuredContent` carries. ([#63](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/63))
- **`openfec_search_contributions` `by_size` and `by_state`** without `committee_id` or `candidate_id` say in their `notice` that each row is one committee's bucket for one cycle. Candidate-scoped calls send `election_full: true`, the upstream default, and echo it in `search_criteria`. ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/66))
- **Legal `url` values are absolute** (`https://www.fec.gov/…`) on `openfec_search_legal` and `openfec_get_legal_document`, where 0.10.1 passed upstream's site-relative paths through; an advisory opinion, which upstream sends without one, gets its fec.gov page. ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/65))
- **An OpenFEC JSON `Query timed out` 504 is attempted once**; 0.10.1 retried it. Every read is capped at 50 s across its retries and 429 waits, so a retry ladder or run of 429 waits that would pass 50 s ends there with a typed error.
- **`openfec_lookup_calendar` upper-cases `state`** and pads a lone digit after a leading `-` (`-1` → `-01`); **`openfec_get_committee_totals` upper-cases `committee_type` and `committee_designation`**, keeping a leading `-`.
- **Server instructions, `openfec-guide` skill (1.2), `campaign-analysis` and `money-trail` prompts, and the election summary note** name donor-scoped contributions, `by_candidate`'s periodic-report scope, and the rows not to sum.

## Removed

- **Output fields**: `truncated`, `shown`, and `cap` on `openfec_search_candidates`, `_contributions`, `_disbursements`, `_expenditures`, `_coordinated_expenditures`, and `_filings`; `truncated`, `shown`, and `nextFromHit` on `openfec_search_legal`; `missing_totals` on `openfec_search_candidates`. Row-level committee records moved to the page, byte-identical: Schedule A `contributor` → `contributor_committees`, Schedule B `recipient_committee` → `recipient_committees`, Schedule E/F `committee` → `committees`. A row whose record differs from the page's (another cycle's, on an all-cycle page) keeps its own. ([#62](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/62), [#63](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/63))

## Fixed

- **A 400, 404, or 422 error names the parameter OpenFEC rejected**, in the message and `content[]`: `OpenFEC rejected committee_type: …`. ([#56](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/56))
- **`openfec_search_expenditures` `by_candidate` returns spenders largest total first**; it returned committee ID order. ([#58](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/58))
- **Elections**: `openfec://election/{cycle}/H/{state}` reads district `00` instead of failing 422; presidential and Senate races take `US` and `00` (`"0"` passes, `"000"` fails); ZIP rows are headed by their race (`GA-05 House 2026`) instead of `Unknown`. ([#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/64))
- **State, party, and committee code inputs are checked before the request.** 0.10.1 checked none of them and sent each upstream as given, so the values below came back empty or unfiltered instead of failing. Rejected: spelled-out or over-wide state and party codes (`Georgia`, `Democratic`); committee type and designation codes outside their sets; a presidential `state` other than `US` or a presidential or Senate `district` other than `00`, without `zip`; a calendar `district` that is not digits; `other` or a leading `-` on a field whose endpoint matches exactly; a bare `-` exclusion. These fail `invalid_state_code`, `invalid_party_code`, `invalid_committee_type`, `invalid_committee_designation`, `inputs_not_applicable_to_office`, or `invalid_district`. A leading `-` (exclude the code) and `other` (foreign, invalid, or missing states) pass on the fields whose endpoint reads them. ([#67](https://github.com/cyanheads/openfec-mcp-server/issues/67))
- **`openfec_search_candidates`** names the refused inputs in `inputs_not_applicable_to_id_lookup`; it said "the named inputs".
- **Legal rendering**: `openfec_search_legal` indented commission-vote and highlight continuation lines six spaces, which CommonMark reads as code blocks that swallowed the following votes (35 on one 200-MUR page), and `openfec_get_legal_document` put each array entry's fields outside its list item, so a vote action's `1.`–`3.` steps joined the section's numbering and later documents ran into one paragraph. Each entry now renders as one list item.
